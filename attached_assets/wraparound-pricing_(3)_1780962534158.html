Common questions

What if I need a feature that isn't in the platform yet?
Submit a feature request or bug report through the platform and tech support will get back to you. Requests that would benefit the broader community of organizations get prioritized on the development roadmap. Wraparound is a living platform — it grows based on the real needs of the people using it, not a spec that was written years ago and never revisited.
What does tech support look like?
Every organization on every plan gets the same dedicated tech support — no tiers, no second-class treatment. For standard issues, you'll receive a response within 24 hours. For critical errors that affect your ability to serve families, we treat those as immediate priority and respond as fast as possible. The families your team coordinates can't wait, and neither can you.
Do lower tiers have fewer features?
No. Every tier gets the complete Wraparound platform — every feature, every dashboard, every tool. The only difference between tiers is the number of active Care Communities your plan supports. We don't believe in locking capabilities behind higher price points. If you're serving families, you deserve the full toolkit regardless of your organization's size.
What counts as a Care Community?
A Care Community is a support team organized around a family — typically including a lead advocate, volunteers, and a coordinator. Only active Care Communities count toward your plan limit. A Care Community is considered active when it is actively being served. Communities can be paused at any time, for any reason, and will not count against your limit until they are unpaused. This means you're never paying for families who are between services or in a holding period.
How is Wraparound different from the platform we've been using?
Wraparound was built specifically for Care Community programs — not adapted from a generic nonprofit or church management tool. Where one-size-fits-all platforms force your workflows into their mold, Wraparound's admin dashboard, family tracking, and reporting are shaped around how Care Community organizations actually operate. If your team has been working around limitations rather than through them, you'll feel the difference quickly.
Can we start on a lower tier and upgrade later?
Absolutely. You can upgrade at any time and we'll prorate the difference. Many organizations start on Seed or Growth while piloting the platform, then move up as their network expands.
Do you offer discounts for nonprofits?
Our pricing is already designed with mission-driven organizations in mind — no per-seat fees, no enterprise markup. For multi-year commitments or coalitions of smaller orgs, reach out and we'll find something that works.
What happens to our data if we cancel?
Your data belongs to your organization. If you cancel, we'll provide a full export of all family records, volunteer information, and activity history in standard formats within 30 days.
We're a statewide network — how does the Network tier work?
Network is a custom annual contract tailored to your scale and rollout timeline. We'll walk through your current infrastructure, projected growth, and integration needs, then propose pricing accordingly. Reach out and we'll schedule a conversation.
