name: Engage positioning
description: Product boundary between Engage and Wraparound for future marketing and implementation.
---

Engage is a standalone church-partnership CRM for foster-care and family-support ministries. Wraparound is not required; optional integration is automatic and two-way.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested that standalone value and optional seamless two-way integration remain prominent. Do not position Engage as a Wraparound-only add-on.

**How to apply:** Preserve this distinction in future product messaging and integration work. A marketing-page request does not authorize building the actual CRM or sync.
